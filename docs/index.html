4-Step Quick Setup (For Non-Technical Users)

1

Check Python Installation

Make sure Python is installed on your PC. Download it free from python.org.

⚠️ CRITICAL STEP: During Python installation, make sure to check the box at the bottom that says "Add python.exe to PATH"!
2

Download & Extract the Zip

Click the Download Setup Package button above. Once downloaded, right-click the file and click Extract All...

3

Run Installer (`install.bat`)

Open the extracted folder and double-click install.bat. This automatically sets up Flask & Playwright for you.

(If Windows Defender shows "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" and then "Run anyway").

4

Launch the App (`run_web_app.bat`)

Double-click run_web_app.bat. Your web browser will open automatically at http://localhost:5000!

frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

❓ Why does my friend need Python installed?
Because the bot runs locally on your PC to control Google Chrome. Python is completely free and takes 1 minute to install. Just remember to check "Add python.exe to PATH" when installing!
❓ What if double-clicking `install.bat` flashes and closes immediately?
This means Python is not installed yet or PATH wasn't checked. Install Python from python.org, make sure to check "Add python.exe to PATH", restart your PC, and double-click install.bat again.
❓ Does Google Sign-in work?
Yes! The app opens your real installed Google Chrome browser. Click "Login with Google", sign in to Naukri, and your session stays saved forever.
❓ Will it skip jobs with questionnaires?
No! The bot has a Smart Answer Engine that auto-fills experience, notice period, CTC, skills, location, and relocation options automatically. It only skips jobs that redirect outside Naukri to company websites.
